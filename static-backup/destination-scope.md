# Destination Collection Scope

## Purpose

Define a consistent destination collection for the nine trip concepts listed in
[`destinations.txt`](./destinations.txt). The collection should help visitors
understand each experience and give them a clear path to inquire or book.

## Destinations in scope

1. Bali Island Escape
2. Krabi Limestone Cruise
3. Maldives Overwater Retreat
4. Malibu Cliff Retreat
5. Modern Desert Haven
6. Patagonia Lake Trek
7. Santorini Private Escape
8. Santorini Sunset Tour
9. Tulum Beach Villa Retreat

## In scope

- Create one destination or experience page for each listed entry.
- Use a consistent page structure: overview, highlights, location, experience
  details, practical information, imagery, and an inquiry or booking call to
  action.
- Distinguish the two Santorini experiences by their audience, itinerary, and
  call to action so they do not read as duplicate listings.
- Organize destinations so visitors can browse and compare the collection.
- Make pages responsive and provide descriptive page titles and summaries.
- Confirm factual details, availability, pricing, images, and booking links
  before publication.

## Out of scope

- Adding destinations not present in the source list.
- Confirming or inventing operators, itineraries, prices, inclusions, or
  availability.
- Building payment, reservation, or customer-account systems.
- Replacing or changing the existing Victoria Falls and African safari content.

## Assumptions and decisions to confirm

- The list represents proposed travel experiences, not verified bookable
  products.
- The current site describes Cuckoo Safaris as a Victoria Falls and southern
  Africa specialist. Confirm whether these international destinations belong
  under that brand or should be presented as a separate collection before
  publishing.
- “Modern Desert Haven” does not identify a specific location; confirm the
  destination before writing location-specific content.
- Confirm whether the primary action should be an inquiry, an external booking
  link, or another existing site action.

## Completion criteria

- All nine entries have a page or approved listing with reviewed content.
- Each page has a distinct title, accurate location and experience details,
  suitable approved imagery, and a working primary action.
- The collection is navigable on mobile and desktop, and the two Santorini
  listings are clearly differentiated.
- Brand fit and all factual, commercial, and booking details are approved
  before launch.
